HOW TO PLAY

CONTROLS

  • WASD or ↑←↓→ drive & turn
  • SPACE or J fire — hold for auto-fire
  • Pressing toward a wall turns without moving, so you can aim. Push into a half-open gap and the tank slides through.
  • P / ESC pause · M mute

MISSION

  • Destroy every enemy in the wave. Lose if the HQ falls or all 3 tanks are lost.
  • Bricks chip half a tile per shot. Steel needs a fully upgraded cannon.
  • Your shells cancel enemy shells — shoot incoming fire.
  • Water stops tanks, not shells. Hide in bushes: enemies can't spot you.
  • Flashing red enemies drop a power-up when hit.
